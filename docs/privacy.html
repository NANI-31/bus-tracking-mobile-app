Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. It is Upasthit's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you across our application and other sites we own and operate.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

Our app requires access to your device's location to provide core functionality, specifically tracking the real-time location of college buses. We may collect location data in the background if you are a driver, to share your position with students and administrators. For students, location data is used to display your position relative to the bus.

Personal Information

We may ask for personal information, such as your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Student/Staff ID

2. Implementation of Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Services used include:

  • Google Maps Services: To provide location-based features and map displays.
  • Firebase: For authentication, database management, and notifications.

3. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@upasthit.app.
